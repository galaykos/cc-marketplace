Ledgerleaf

One place for the 401(k), the health plan and the leave ledger.

Every benefit your company offers, kept in one record, so the answer an employee gets is the answer payroll already has.

Who it is for

Built for the 429 teams of fifty to five hundred people who have outgrown a spreadsheet and do not yet want a broker on retainer.

Why change

Open enrolment arrives and the answer lives in four places at once. Payroll holds one number, the broker in Suite 502 holds another, and the handbook on the shared drive holds a third that nobody has opened since the plan changed. The employee asks HR, HR asks the broker, and the week goes. The cost of the gap is not the premium — it is the fortnight of answering the same question by hand.

How it works

  1. Connect payroll: Ledgerleaf reads GET /v1/employees nightly with a scoped api key.
  2. Map each plan to the roster it covers, once.
  3. Employees see their own record; HR sees the roll-up.

Limits, and what this is not
