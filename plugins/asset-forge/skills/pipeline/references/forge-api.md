# forge API

Generated by `scripts/gen_forge_api.py` from the module sources (signature + first docstring sentence).
Live modules run through `execute_blender_code` after the import snippet; headless ones are reached
through the forge MCP tools (`validate_asset`, `bake_maps`, …) or `batch_run`. Distances are metres.

## blockout (live)

- `skin_from_skeleton(spec, name='Blockout', subdivisions=1)` — Skin a joint graph into a closed tube mesh.
- `metaball_blob(spec, name='Blob', resolution=0.1)` — Fuse metaball elements into one mesh.
- `rock(seed=0, size=1.0, style='boulder', name='SM_Rock')` — A procedural rock: a noise-bumped, stretched icosphere cut by random planes (capped and triangulated).
- `lathe(profile_points, segments=16, name='Lathe')` — Revolve a profile in the XZ plane about the Z axis.
- `loft(cross_sections, name='Loft', closed=True)` — Skin quads between consecutive cross-sections, each a list of [x, y, z] points (metres) of the same length.

## deform (live)

- `grab(obj, center, radius, offset, falloff='smooth', symmetry=None, mask=None)` — Move vertices within `radius` metres of world `center` by world `offset` (metres) scaled by the falloff.
- `inflate(obj, center, radius, amount, falloff='smooth', symmetry=None, mask=None)` — Push vertices within `radius` metres of world `center` along their normals by `amount` metres (negative deflates) scaled by the falloff.
- `pinch(obj, center, radius, strength, falloff='smooth', symmetry=None, mask=None)` — Pull vertices within `radius` metres of world `center` toward it along the surface (the part of the pull along the vertex normal is dropped).
- `flatten(obj, center, radius, plane=None, strength=1.0, falloff='smooth', symmetry=None, mask=None)` — Move vertices within `radius` metres of world `center` toward a plane by `strength` (0..1) of their distance, scaled by the falloff.
- `crease_along_curve(obj, curve_points, width, depth, sharpness=2.0, falloff='smooth', symmetry=None, mask=None)` — Cut a groove along a world-space polyline: vertices within `width` metres of it move inward along their normals by `depth` metres (negative raises a ridge) times falloff(t) ** sharpness, t = distance / width.
- `bulge_along_curve(obj, curve_points, radius_profile, amount, falloff='smooth', symmetry=None, mask=None)` — Raise a ridge along a world-space polyline (muscles, veins, roots): vertices move along their normals by `amount` metres times falloff(distance / R), where R (metres) is `radius_profile` interpolated by arc length (its values are spaced evenly from the first point to the last).
- `smooth(obj, iterations=1, factor=0.5, mask=None)` — Laplacian smoothing over edge neighbours: each pass moves a vertex `factor` (0..1] of the way to the mean of its neighbours.
- `noise_displace(obj, scale, strength, octaves=3, seed=0, mask=None)` — Displace every vertex along its normal by fractal Perlin noise (mathutils.noise.fractal) sampled at its world position.
- `stroke(obj, path_points, op, radius_profile, strength_profile, spacing=None, **op_kwargs)` — Apply op ("grab", "inflate", "pinch" or "flatten") as dabs every `spacing` metres along a world-space path, like a sculpt stroke.
- `experimental_brush_stroke(obj, path_points, pressure=1.0, mode='NORMAL')` — EXPERIMENTAL: run the active sculpt brush along a world-space path with a real sculpt-mode stroke (bpy.ops.sculpt.brush_stroke) in the first visible 3D View, then return to the previous mode.

## uv (live)

- `smart_project(obj, angle_deg=66.0, margin=0.01)` — Smart UV Project every face of a mesh.
- `seams_from_sharp(obj, angle_deg=30.0)` — Mark a UV seam on every edge whose two faces meet at more than angle_deg (degrees between normals) and on every edge already marked sharp.
- `unwrap(obj, method='ANGLE_BASED')` — Unwrap every face along the mesh's marked seams with the uv.unwrap operator.
- `pack(obj, margin_px=4, texture_px=1024)` — Pack the active UV map's islands into 0-1 with a gap of margin_px texture pixels at texture_px square (margin in UV units = margin_px / texture_px).
- `texel_density(obj, texture_px)` — Measured texel density of the active UV map in pixels per metre at texture_px square: per-face density weighted by world-space face area.
- `set_texel_density(obj, px_per_m, texture_px)` — Scale the active UV map uniformly about its bounding-box centre so the measured texel density becomes px_per_m (pixels per metre) at texture_px square.
- `checker_material(obj, texture_px=1024)` — Show a generated UV-grid checker on obj.

## lod (live)

- `make_lod_chain(obj, ratios=None, engine='unity', platform='pc', protect=PROTECT_ALL)` — Build LOD1+ as collapse-decimated copies of obj at each ratio of LOD0's evaluated triangle count.

## collision (live)

- `make_collision(obj, kind='convex', engine='unity', max_hulls=8, max_hull_verts=HULL_VERT_CAP, parent=True)` — Build collision for a render mesh from its evaluated geometry, in its object space.

## retarget (live)

- `load_bonemap(name_or_dict)` — Load presets/bonemaps/<name>.json, or check a dict of the same shape (or a load_bonemap result).
- `retarget(source_rig, target_rig, bonemap, action=None, frame_range=None, use_location_for=('root',))` — Bake `action` (default: the source's active action) from source_rig onto target_rig through `bonemap`.
- `make_in_place(action, root_bone, axes=('X', 'Y'), rig=None)` — Zero root_bone's travel along the world `axes` (default the horizontal pair; Blender is Z-up) and keep the others, such as the vertical bob.
- `extract_root_motion(rig, action, hips_bone, root_bone, create=False)` — Move the hips' horizontal (world X/Y) travel, in metres, onto root_bone: per whole frame of the action, the root gains the hips' horizontal offset from where the root carries the hips' rest position, and the hips lose it, so the hips' world path is unchanged.
- `loop_cleanup(action, blend_frames=2, travel_bones=())` — Make the action's last frame equal its first and add a Cycles modifier to every F-curve.
- `trim(action, start, end)` — Keep the keys between frames start and end (adding a key at each cut with the curve's value there) and shift them so the clip starts at frame 0, not 1: glTF writes time = frame / fps, so a clip at 0 exports from 0 s without export_anim_slide_to_zero (blender plugin's animation.md).
- `push_to_nla(rig, action, track_name=None, engine='unity', asset=None, clip=None)` — Name the action per the engine preset's animation.clip_naming ({asset} defaults to the rig's name, {clip} to the action's), add the preset's loop_suffix when the action loops (a Cycles modifier from loop_cleanup), push it onto its own NLA track (once; a second call renames in place) with the rig's slot, and clear the active action as Push Down does: FBX with All Actions off ships strips only.

## util (live)

- `ok(**fields)` — The summary dict every public forge function returns.
- `blender_at_least(major, minor=0)` — .
- `get_object(name_or_obj, kind=None)` — Return the object, or raise ForgeError.
- `load_preset(kind, name)` — kind: 'engines', 'budgets' or 'bonemaps'.
- `ensure_object_mode()` — .
- `select_only(*objects, active=None)` — Deselect everything, select `objects`, make `active` (default: the first) active.
- `edit_bmesh(obj)` — Yield a bmesh of obj's base mesh (no modifiers); written back and freed on exit.
- `evaluated_bmesh(obj)` — A bmesh of obj with modifiers applied, in object space.
- `tri_count(obj, evaluated=True)` — Triangles the engine will receive: sum of (corners - 2) per face.
- `world_bbox(*objects)` — (min Vector, max Vector) of the objects' evaluated bounding boxes in world space.
- `link_to_scene(obj, collection=None)` — .
- `new_mesh_object(name, bm=None, collection=None)` — Create a mesh object (from a bmesh when given, which is not freed) and link it.
- `apply_transforms(obj, location=False, rotation=True, scale=True)` — Bake scale, then rotation, then location into a mesh's data without bpy.ops; children keep their world pose.
- `material(name, color=(0.8, 0.8, 0.8, 1.0))` — Get or create a node material whose Principled BSDF base colour is `color` (linear RGBA).
- `settable_enum(owner, prop, wanted)` — Return the first identifier in `wanted` that `owner.prop` accepts at run time, else None.

## bake (headless, via the MCP tools)

- `bake_maps(low, out_dir, high=None, maps=MAPS, resolution=1024, extrusion=0.02, cage=None, samples=16, margin_px=8)` — Bake `maps` of `low` (object or name) into `<out_dir>/<low>_<map>.png`, one image per map across all of its material slots, through a temporary image node per material (removed afterwards).

## stylize (headless, via the MCP tools)

- `stylize(obj, out_dir, palette=None, recipe='stone', resolution=1024, maps=None)` — Paint a hand-painted albedo for `obj` (object or name) and assign it as one simple material on every slot.
- `apply_albedo(obj, albedo_path, material_name=None, roughness=0.85)` — Live step after a headless stylize or projection: merge `obj` to ONE slot holding one material whose base colour is the PNG at `albedo_path` (sRGB) — the per-slot colours are already in the albedo, and one slot is one draw call.

## project_paint (headless, via the MCP tools)

- `setup_views(obj, views=('front', 'back', 'left', 'right', 'top'), resolution=1024)` — Create or update one orthographic camera per view, framing `obj` (object or name) with a 10% margin.
- `render_guides(obj, out_dir, cameras)` — Render normal, depth and AO guides of `obj` alone from each camera into `<out_dir>/<obj>_<view>_<guide>.png` (RGBA, transparent background, `forge_resolution` px square, else 1024).
- `project_and_bake(obj, view_images, out_dir, resolution=1024, seam_blend=0.2)` — Project each view image from its camera onto `obj` (object or name) and bake the blend into its own UVs as `<out_dir>/<obj>_projected.png` (`resolution` px square, RGBA, alpha 1).
- `prepare_views(obj, out_dir, views=('front', 'back', 'left', 'right', 'top'), resolution=1024)` — setup_views then render_guides in one call, for a headless job that saves the cameras with a copy of the file.

## review_renders (headless, via the MCP tools)

- `render_review_set(obj, out_dir, angles=8, include=KINDS, game_camera=None, tile_px=512)` — Render the review set of a mesh object into out_dir as PNGs and tile them into out_dir/contact_sheet.png.

## validate (headless, via the MCP tools)

- `validate_asset(obj, engine='unity', platform='pc', asset_class='prop_medium', overrides=None, expected_size_m=None)` — Check a mesh object against presets/budgets/<platform>.json and presets/engines/<engine>.json.

## export (headless, via the MCP tools)

- `export_asset(objects, engine='unity', out_dir=None, file_stem=None, include_lods=True, include_collision=True, platform='pc')` — Export objects (names or objects; the first is the asset) with their LODs and collision, found by the engine preset's naming patterns, through the preset's exporter (FBX or glTF).
