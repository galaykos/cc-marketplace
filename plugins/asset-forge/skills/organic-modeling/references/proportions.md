# Proportions cheat-sheet

Head units (H = head height, chin to crown). Heights in metres for a game character standing in rest pose.

| Style | Height in H | Head H | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Realistic adult | 7.5 | 0.23 m at 1.75 m | Shoulders 2 H wide (male), hips 1.5 H; elbow at waist, wrist at crotch; crotch at 4 H from the floor |
| Heroic | 8 | 0.22 m at 1.8 m | Longer legs, shoulders 2.3 H |
| Stylized | 5–6 | 0.3–0.35 m | Bigger head and hands, shorter legs; keep the wrist at about crotch height so animations still read |
| Chibi / super-deformed | 2–3 | head ≈ body | Arms reach only past the hips; legs 1 H or less; feet oversized |

Faces (front view, eyes on the vertical midline of the head in realistic, lower in stylized):

| Feature | Realistic | Stylized |
|---|---|---|
| Eye line | 1/2 H | 0.55–0.6 H from the crown (big forehead) |
| Eye spacing | one eye width apart | 1–1.5 eye widths |
| Nose bottom | 3/4 H | small or omitted |
| Mouth | 1/3 between nose and chin | close to the nose |
| Ears | eye line to nose bottom | same, often larger |

Quadrupeds: chest deeper than the hips; front legs under the shoulder blades, not under the neck; the back of the
hind leg bends backward at the hock (the "knee" seen from behind is the ankle). Torso length ≈ 2.5–3 head lengths
for a canine, 3.5–4 for a horse.

Game readability: at the game camera distance, silhouette features under about 4 px disappear — exaggerate horns,
ears, weapon heads and hands by 10–30% over realistic.
