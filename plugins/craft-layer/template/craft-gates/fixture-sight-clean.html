Daily spend ceiling

Every card on the account shares one ceiling. The chart reads the week against it, so an overrun is a shape rather than a number to look up.

Thursday clears the line by a little under nine per cent. The week still closes inside the ceiling, because Wednesday ran light enough to pay for it.

A ceiling is not a budget. It stops a card, it does not plan a month, and a week spent hugging it is a week with no room for the thing nobody forecast.

Raising it is a two-signature change and takes effect at the next reconciliation, never retroactively.

Reconciliation runs at 04:00. Anything settled after that lands on the next day's ledger, which is why a late Friday can read as a Monday overrun.
